Delivery, Revisions & Project Scope

Updated November 18, 2026 · Contact: bestfuturestudio@gmail.com

1. Standard deliverables

Resume Refresh — $79: one professionally edited and formatted resume based on verified customer information. Career Essentials — $119: one resume and one tailored cover letter for a specified role. Social Starter — $99: five promotional graphics and five matching captions. Marketing Boost — $179: ten promotional graphics, ten captions and one promotional flyer. Unless stated in a written quote, packages do not include multiple alternative design directions, account posting, printing, licensed stock purchases or editable source files.

2. Delivery targets and when the clock starts

Resume Refresh: target 24 hours; Career Essentials: 24–48 hours; Social Starter: 48 hours; Marketing Boost: 72 hours. These are estimated production windows, not absolute deadlines. The production clock begins only after successful payment, receipt of a complete intake, usable source materials, and resolution of necessary clarifications. Customer changes or missing information may pause or restart the target by mutual agreement. We will communicate material delays and provide remedies required by law.

3. Submission requirements

Resume customers should provide accurate employment history, education, accomplishments and target job information. Marketing customers should provide business details, target audience, approved promotional claims, brand assets and any required usage rights. Share documents through restricted links; do not submit Social Security numbers, card details, passwords, medical records or unrelated confidential information.

4. Review and revision process

Initial delivery will be sent to the order email or an access-controlled link. Each package includes one consolidated revision round, requested within seven calendar days after initial delivery, limited to the original scope. A single round means one collected set of changes, not unlimited iterative edits. We will correct our own formatting and transcription errors as appropriate. New job targets, new marketing campaigns, additional graphics or substantive new requirements may incur an additional quoted charge only with customer approval.

5. Customer approval and responsibility

Customers must review names, dates, qualifications, measurable claims, contact information, URLs, spelling, logos and advertising statements before use. We cannot verify all external claims independently. We will not invent credentials, outcomes or endorsements. Customers control the final use and publication of delivered content.

6. Delivery records and technical issues

We maintain reasonable records of order scope, payment verification, work commencement, communications, file versions and delivery confirmation to resolve support requests and payment disputes. If a file cannot be opened, notify us and we will attempt a compatible replacement. We will not claim delivery merely because a file was drafted internally.

7. Changes and cancellation

See Refund & Cancellation Policy. Its 24-hour and 72-hour cancellation windows are measured from payment time, whereas production targets begin after complete intake. These are different clocks. Customers should consider this before purchasing or delaying intake.

These policies are subject to applicable law. They do not waive statutory consumer rights or cardholder dispute rights. If a term is unenforceable, the remainder remains in effect to the extent permitted by law. Changes apply prospectively; terms presented at checkout govern that order unless law requires otherwise.
