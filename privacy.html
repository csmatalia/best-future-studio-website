Privacy Policy

Updated November 18, 2026 · Contact: bestfuturestudio@gmail.com

1. Scope and information collected

This notice covers information provided through our website, email, Stripe checkout and Google Forms intake. Depending on the project, we may receive names, email addresses, billing references, resume and employment history, education, target jobs, business names, brand assets, promotional information, messages, document links and project feedback. We ask customers not to submit Social Security numbers, full payment card details, government identification numbers, passwords or unrelated sensitive records.

2. Purposes of processing

We use information to answer inquiries, confirm and fulfill orders, communicate about projects, prepare requested documents, provide revisions, address refunds and disputes, protect against fraud, maintain accounting records and comply with applicable legal duties. We do not sell customer resumes or business briefs and do not use them for public testimonials without permission.

3. Providers and sharing

Our static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We use Google Forms and Sheets for intake and order tracking, Google Drive for restricted file sharing where used, Gmail for communications and Stripe for checkout and payment records. These providers may process data under their own privacy and security terms. We share only what is reasonably necessary with service providers, authorized personnel, and authorities when legally required. We do not ask customers to enter payment card numbers into Google Forms.

4. AI-assisted work

We may use digital writing, editing or design tools, including AI-assisted tools, to prepare work. We will not intentionally submit unnecessary confidential or sensitive customer information to such tools. Where a proposed tool would require uploading private client documents, we will assess the provider's data practices and seek appropriate permission where required. Human review is used for factual accuracy and suitability; AI-generated outputs can contain errors and are not guaranteed unique.

5. Storage, security and restricted links

We use account access controls and restrict customer document sharing to authorized recipients where reasonably practicable. Customers should share only necessary documents using restricted access rather than public links. Email and cloud services carry security risks; no system is completely secure. If a suspected incident affects personal information, we will investigate and provide notifications as required by applicable law.

6. Retention and deletion requests

Project files are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for fulfillment, revisions, support, disputes and recordkeeping. We aim to review and remove unneeded working copies within 90 days after project closure where practicable, while transaction, tax, dispute and legally required records may be kept longer. This is an operational target, not a guarantee of deletion from provider backups. Customers can request access, correction or deletion at bestfuturestudio@gmail.com; we will verify requests appropriately and explain lawful retention exceptions.

7. Cookies, analytics and external links

Our current static site does not intentionally embed advertising trackers. GitHub, Google, Stripe and linked services may use cookies, technical logs and analytics according to their own notices. We will review and update this policy before adding third-party marketing trackers or mailing-list tools.

8. Children and marketing messages

Our services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information. We do not automatically enroll intake customers into promotional mailing lists; any future marketing email program will include applicable consent and unsubscribe mechanisms.

9. Rights, updates and contact

Privacy rights vary by jurisdiction. Send inquiries to bestfuturestudio@gmail.com; include only the information necessary to locate your order. We will review requests under applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date and applied prospectively where appropriate.

These policies are subject to applicable law. They do not waive statutory consumer rights or cardholder dispute rights. If a term is unenforceable, the remainder remains in effect to the extent permitted by law. Changes apply prospectively; terms presented at checkout govern that order unless law requires otherwise.
