Refund & Cancellation Policy

Updated November 18, 2026 · Contact: bestfuturestudio@gmail.com

This policy applies to one-time purchases of customized digital services. Please review the schedule before checkout. A cancellation request must be emailed to bestfuturestudio@gmail.com and is measured by the time it reaches our mailbox.

1. Cancellation within 24 hours

Cancel within 24 elapsed hours after successful payment and receive a full refund of the service price, even if work has started. This is our voluntary customer cancellation benefit and does not reduce statutory rights.

2. After 24 hours and before final delivery

If substantive project work has begun, we retain a cancellation charge of 20% of the original service price and refund the remaining 80%. If substantive work has not begun, we refund 100% of the service price when cancellation is received before 72 hours. “Work begun” means project-specific research, writing, editing, design or production—not merely receiving payment or an automated form response. We will maintain a dated work record if a charge is disputed.

3. After completed work is delivered

Once we have completed and delivered all purchased deliverables in accordance with the agreed project scope, change-of-mind cancellation refunds are not offered. Submission of customer requirements alone does not mean our work is completed or delivered. This policy does not eliminate statutory rights or remedies for non-delivery, defective work, material nonconformity, duplicate charges or unauthorized payments. The included revision round remains available under the Delivery & Revision Policy.

4. How time and amounts are calculated

The initial 24-hour cancellation window begins at the timestamp of successful payment recorded by Stripe and includes weekends and holidays. After that window, cancellation treatment depends on whether substantive project work has begun and whether all agreed deliverables have been completed and delivered. We maintain dated records of work commencement and delivery. The 20% cancellation charge is based on the service price, not separately collected taxes that must be returned by law. Cancellation timing is determined by receipt of the customer's email, not when we respond.

5. After delivery, quality concerns and corrections

Completed custom services are generally not refundable for a change of mind outside the cancellation window. Customers may request their included revision within seven calendar days of initial delivery. If work materially fails to match the purchased description, or we made an error, we will review and offer a reasonable correction, redelivery, partial refund or full refund as required by law and the circumstances. We do not use the completed-delivery rule to deny legitimate non-delivery claims.

6. Billing mistakes, duplicates and unauthorized charges

Contact us promptly regarding duplicate charges or incorrect amounts. We will investigate and correct verified billing errors. Customers retain all rights to contact their card issuer or payment provider for unauthorized transactions and applicable disputes.

7. How to request cancellation

Email bestfuturestudio@gmail.com with subject “Cancellation Request,” your order email, package, approximate payment date and a brief request. Do not provide card numbers, passwords or government ID numbers. We aim to acknowledge the request within two business days. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method; Stripe and the issuing bank determine posting times. Original processing fees or dispute fees may still be charged to us by the payment provider.

8. Examples

For a $119 service canceled at hour 36 after substantive work began and before final delivery, the cancellation charge is $23.80 and the service-price refund is $95.20. For the same order canceled at hour 12, the service-price refund is $119. For a request after completed work has been delivered, no change-of-mind refund is offered, subject to the exceptions above.

These policies are subject to applicable law. They do not waive statutory consumer rights or cardholder dispute rights. If a term is unenforceable, the remainder remains in effect to the extent permitted by law. Changes apply prospectively; terms presented at checkout govern that order unless law requires otherwise.
