Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026 · Contact: bestfuturestudio@gmail.com

Cancellation within the first 24 hours

Customers may cancel within 24 hours of placing an order and receive a full refund of the service price, even if work has already begun. The cancellation request must be received by us within that 24-hour period.

Cancellation after 24 hours but before 72 hours

If work has begun and we receive your cancellation request more than 24 hours but less than 72 hours after the order was placed, a cancellation charge equal to 20% of the original service price applies. We refund the remaining 80% of the service price. If work has not begun, we refund the full service price for cancellations received before the 72-hour cutoff.

Cancellation at or after 72 hours

Cancellation requests received 72 hours or more after the original order was placed are not eligible for a change-of-mind refund, including where delivery has already occurred. This rule does not limit rights or remedies required by applicable law or remedies for services not provided as agreed.

How the deadline is calculated

The cancellation window starts at the recorded time of successful payment for the order. Twenty-four hours means 24 elapsed hours, and three days means 72 elapsed hours, including weekends and holidays. The time we receive your cancellation email determines which window applies.

Problems with an order

For duplicate charges, billing mistakes, missing services, or work that materially differs from the agreed scope, contact us so we can investigate and provide an appropriate correction, revised delivery, or refund consistent with applicable law. Your included revision rights remain available under our Delivery & Revision Policy.

How to cancel or request a refund

Email bestfuturestudio@gmail.com with the subject “Cancellation Request,” your order email, package, payment date, and request. Do not include payment card numbers. We aim to acknowledge requests within two business days; eligibility is based on when your email was received, not when we respond. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method; financial institutions control posting times. The 20% cancellation charge is calculated on the original service price, excluding taxes that must be refunded under applicable law.

These terms apply to Best Future Studio's described services. If a provision conflicts with applicable law, applicable law controls. We may update this page prospectively and will identify material changes where reasonably practicable.
